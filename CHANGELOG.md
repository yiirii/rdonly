# Changelog

## 0.4.0 — 2026-11-23
A library you can organise.
- **Continue reading:** a big card for the book you were last in, tinted to match its cover. Resume drops you straight back in.
- **Tabs:** All · Reading · Not started · Finished, then your own collections. A book can sit in as many collections as you like.
- **Collections:** make one with **+**, give it a colour, rename, recolour or delete it (right-click on the laptop, press and hold on the phone), drag tabs to reorder.
- **Find things fast:** search titles, authors, series and your own tags (press / on the laptop); type chips to show only manga, comics and so on; sort by recently added, recently read or title.
- **Progress on every cover**, turning green when you finish. Mark a book finished (or not) yourself from its menu.
- **Select several books** (Ctrl/Shift-click, or Select from a book's menu) to add them to a collection, tag them or delete them together. Drag covers onto a tab to add them.
- **Cover size slider**, and a clear Settings button.
- On the phone the Continue card scrolls away and the search and tabs stay pinned at the top.
- Playful touches: shapes that bounce in the Continue card, a squishy tab highlight, covers that tilt in, chips that shrink to dots while filtering, and a little burst of squares when you make a collection. All calm down to simple fades if your device asks for reduced motion.
- Fixed: tapping right after closing a sheet sometimes did nothing.

## 0.3.0 — 2026-11-22
Reading that looks the way you want.
- **Seven curated fonts:** Newsreader (new default), Vollkorn, Spectral, Instrument Sans, Funnel Sans, Host Grotesk, Atkinson Hyperlegible — plus the book's own. All work offline.
- **14 page colours:** Paper, Linen, Sepia, Parchment, Rose, Sage, Mist, Dusk, Slate, Night, Ink, Forest, Ember, OLED — every one kept comfortable on the eyes.
- **New Aa sheet:** colours, fonts (shown in their own typeface), text size, line spacing, margins, and a two-column switch on the laptop. On the laptop it sits on the right so you can watch the page change.
- **Page-turn styles:** Glide (default), Fold (a flat sheet sweeps across) or Scroll (read a chapter as one long page). Each device keeps its own choice.
- Book titles in the library now use Newsreader.

## 0.2.1 — 2026-11-22
- **Proper Windows installer:** replaces any older version when you install, keeps your library, and puts a Rd Only icon on the desktop.
- **Reader bars appear when the mouse nears the top or bottom edge,** and stay while you're over them.
- **Smoother novel page turns:** a short slide-and-fade on click, tap or arrow key; swipes on the phone still follow your finger.
- **Series folders:** books with the same Series become one stacked folder showing the count; inside, volumes are in A–Z order.
- **Import menu** on the laptop: "Books…" or "Folder of images…" (every picture in a folder becomes one comic).
- New app icon.

## 0.2.0 — 2026-11-22
Books open now.
- Tap a cover to read; the book grows out of its cover. Back (or Escape) returns to the library and your place is saved.
- **Novels (EPUB):** real pages, two columns on the laptop, tap the edges or use the arrow keys to turn. Aa sheet with four page colours (Paper, Sepia, Night, OLED), three book fonts plus the book's own, and text size. Chapter and a slider in the bottom bar.
- **Manga:** right-to-left pages; two pages side by side on the laptop.
- **Comics:** left-to-right pages, same as manga otherwise.
- **Webtoons:** one smooth vertical strip; tall-page comics are spotted automatically.
- **PDFs:** continuous scrolling pages, fit to width.
- Pinch, double-tap or Ctrl+scroll to zoom comics and PDFs.
- CBR and CB7 files, and (laptop) a folder of images as one comic.
- Long-press (phone) or right-click (laptop) a cover: edit details, change type, delete.
- Switch a comic between manga, comic and webtoon reading from inside the reader.
- New app icon.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-21
First build of Rd Only.
- Library with cover grid, newest-first or A–Z sorting.
- Import EPUB, PDF and CBZ files (button, or drag and drop on the laptop). Titles, authors, series and covers are read from the files.
- The same file is never added twice; a lost file can be re-imported to restore it.
- Books are kept in the app's own storage. On iPhone the app asks to keep that storage permanently, and Settings shows whether it agreed.
- Works offline on iPhone and updates itself quietly when a new version is published.
- Laptop: portable Windows app (no install step).
