# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — 2026-11-22
Books open now.
- Tap a cover to read; the book grows out of its cover. Back (or Escape) returns to the library and your place is saved.
- **Novels (EPUB):** real pages, two columns on the laptop, tap the edges or use the arrow keys to turn. Aa sheet with four page colours (Paper, Sepia, Night, OLED), three book fonts plus the book's own, and text size. Chapter and a slider in the bottom bar.
- **Manga:** right-to-left pages; two pages side by side on the laptop.
- **Comics:** left-to-right pages, same as manga otherwise.
- **Webtoons:** one smooth vertical strip; tall-page comics are spotted automatically.
- **PDFs:** continuous scrolling pages, fit to width.
- Pinch, double-tap or Ctrl+scroll to zoom comics and PDFs.
- CBR and CB7 files, and (laptop) a folder of images as one comic.
- Long-press (phone) or right-click (laptop) a cover: edit details, change type, delete.
- Switch a comic between manga, comic and webtoon reading from inside the reader.
- New app icon.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-21
First build of Rd Only.
- Library with cover grid, newest-first or A–Z sorting.
- Import EPUB, PDF and CBZ files (button, or drag and drop on the laptop). Titles, authors, series and covers are read from the files.
- The same file is never added twice; a lost file can be re-imported to restore it.
- Books are kept in the app's own storage. On iPhone the app asks to keep that storage permanently, and Settings shows whether it agreed.
- Works offline on iPhone and updates itself quietly when a new version is published.
- Laptop: portable Windows app (no install step).
